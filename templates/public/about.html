Our Mission

Closing the Loop on Global Electronic Waste

EcoCycle is an integrated digital platform that connects households, verified local collectors, accredited recycling facilities, and green manufacturing buyers into one transparent, traceable circular lifecycle.

Our Guiding Principles
  • Zero-Landfill Commitment

    Every gram of collected e-waste is accounted for and recovered without landfill dumping.

  • Empowering Local Collectors

    Equipping local logistics collectors with mobile scheduling, fair remuneration, and route tracking.

  • Circular B2B Supply Chain

    Supplying verified high-purity recycled copper, rare earth elements, and tested refurbished hardware back to industries.
