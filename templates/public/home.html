How EcoCycle Works

A seamless, fully transparent 5-step journey from your doorstep to high-grade industrial circular resources.

01
Upload E-Waste

Snap a photo of your discarded laptop, phone, or appliance and submit details with your preferred pickup time.

02
Admin Verification

Our operational team verifies your submission, awards you Eco-Points, and assigns a verified local collector.

03
Collector Pickup

An eco-transport collector visits your location, verifies item weight, captures proof photos, and collects your items.

04
Recycling & Refining

Materials are sorted, tested, dismantled, and processed into high-purity copper, gold, circuit boards, and polymers.

05
Marketplace B2B

Refurbished hardware and raw recovered materials are published to our B2B marketplace for corporate buyers.

Environmental Responsibility

Why Recycle E-Waste With Us?

Electronic waste represents 70% of overall toxic waste in landfills. Together, we can redirect valuable resources.

Reduce Toxic Pollution

Prevent hazardous lead, cadmium, and mercury from leaching into groundwater and agricultural soil systems.

Recover Precious Metals

1 metric ton of circuit boards contains 40 to 800 times more gold and 30 to 40 times more copper than one ton of mined ore.

Support Circular Economy

Refurbished components and purified materials re-enter commercial production, reducing virgin resource extraction.
